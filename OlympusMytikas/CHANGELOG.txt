Olympus Mytikas — 1.1.2

1.1.2 — fixes for WoW Forever
The shared window shows your installed version and a selectable Member Home address alongside the KoS toggle.
Forever map pins use the correct map coordinates. Recruit keeps saved profiles, fits its ads in the chat limit, and checks for your own message in each channel.
The debt Ledger follows the current bounty on each target. Deleting or resetting targets removes their Ledger rows. Starting a new bounty on a settled target replaces its previous Ledger row.
Guildmates can sync large boards and offscreen targets. The monthly prize rolls over in UTC and saved payouts survive a board reset.

Also included from the 1g update

1.1.0 — major update: live 1g bounties
Live bounties: right-click, /kos bounty, /kos fund or Fund puts up 1g at once; requester owes until GM/Valar runs /kos paidup <name>. /kos owed, Ledger and Officers show it.
Every bounty shows the killer's level and the layer he was on (layer S, layer P) on the board row, detail panel, Announce and /kos where. Read from LayerSwap and visible creatures, never guessed.
70/30 stays: bank keeps 30%, hunter's 70% joins the month's one prize pool, top hunter wins, a person pays, /kos boardpaid records it. Addon never moves gold.

1.1.1 — small follow-up
New Ledger tab: who owes what, plus cleared debts with date and who cleared them.
Fixes in this build: no mouse-wheel scrolling (use Prev/Next), board and Ledger rows stay still during sync, old 3g rows cannot reset 1g, plain words: bank keeps, prize paid out, prize pool, refunded.
Layers show only on NEW kills and retire after one hour; unknown stays unknown, your own layer is read only at a real death. Announce reads latest level/layer first.
Swap to layer opens a plain RP whisper to the victim who set the bounty; press Enter. Hidden LayerSwap notes still let hosts auto-invite.
Only the guild master or Valar can delete; deletes stay final on old and new clients. Re-add by hand (/kos add).
Kill/death notices no longer spam chat - /kos chat replays them, /kos chat clear wipes them.
The window has a KoS ON/OFF button; /kos off|on does the same. Off stops notices, sync, badges and pins.
All Olympus-family local chat is hidden to cut spam; /mytikas hidden replays it. Replies to commands you type still show.
First-run notice covers all of the above in 7 steps.

Olympus Mytikas 1.0.0 - the two addons are now one

Install one folder. Inside it: OlympusRecruit 1.6.5 + KillOnSight 1.5.4 in one shell window with two tabs, one minimap button, their bundled map libraries and the Olympus art.

WHAT'S IN THIS ONE ADDON
- Files: OlympusMytikas.toc + OlympusMytikas.lua (the shared window, tabs and minimap button), OlympusRecruit.lua, KillOnSight.lua, Bootstrap.lua, libs\ (LibStub, CallbackHandler, HereBeDragons + pins), Media\ (portrait, minimap, panel, glow, bounty, emblem), CHANGELOG.txt.
- Recruit tab: /om posts one random ad to /1 General and /2 Trade in one pass, 61 ads, custom ads (/om add, /om del), and the right-click "Invite to <guild>" line. Forever forbids addon invites, so it copies /ginvite First Surname for you to paste and press yourself. No auto-post.
- Kill on Sight tab: shared kill board, death feed with last-known locations, 30-minute map pins, pending bounty jobs, flat 3g bounties verified against the guild bank, 70/30 split, monthly Hunter Board with one pooled prize, first-run rules notice, audit log, Valar/GM-only remove and settle.
- Shared skin: one window on Forever's own chrome, titled Olympus Mytikas, with the white-mountain-on-navy portrait and matching minimap button (left opens the last tab, right posts an ad, drag saves the angle). KoS keeps its Board / Hunter Board sub-tabs; all /om and /kos commands still work.

NEW IN THE MERGE
- The Recruit tab's old custom art is gone; both tabs use the kill board's frame and bronze insets.
- Latest upstream fixes are in: Recruit 1.6.5 dropped the 15 guild-war ads; KoS 1.5.4 fixed the "Valar" rank spelling that locked the rank out of its own board.

STILL TRUE
Gold never moves by addon: a person deposits at the bank and pays the hunter. A name on the board is not a bounty until the bank balance proves it.
Delete the old OlympusRecruit and KillOnSight folders after installing. The source addons are left untouched for reference.
