Olympus Mytikas — 1.1.1

Since the 1g update

1.1.0 — major update: live 1g bounties
Live bounties: right-click, /kos bounty, /kos fund or Fund puts up 1g at once; requester owes until GM/Valar runs /kos paidup <name>. /kos owed, Ledger and Officers show it.
Every bounty shows the killer's level and the layer he was on (layer S, layer P) on the board row, detail panel, Announce and /kos where. Read from LayerSwap and visible creatures, never guessed.
70/30 stays: bank keeps 30%, hunter's 70% joins the month's one prize pool, top hunter wins, a person pays, /kos boardpaid records it. Addon never moves gold.

1.1.1 — small follow-up
New Ledger tab: who owes what, plus cleared debts with date and who cleared them.
Fixes in this build: no mouse-wheel scrolling (use Prev/Next), board and Ledger rows stay still during sync, old 3g rows cannot reset 1g, plain words: bank keeps, prize paid out, prize pool, refunded.
Layers show only on NEW kills and retire after one hour; unknown stays unknown, your own layer is read only at a real death. Announce reads latest level/layer first.
Swap to layer opens a plain RP whisper to the victim who set the bounty; press Enter. Hidden LayerSwap notes still let hosts auto-invite.
Only the guild master or Valar can delete; deletes stay final on old and new clients. Re-add by hand (/kos add).
Kill/death notices no longer spam chat - /kos chat replays them, /kos chat clear wipes them.
The window has a KoS ON/OFF button; /kos off|on does the same. Off stops notices, sync, badges and pins.
All Olympus-family local chat is hidden to cut spam; /mytikas hidden replays it. Replies to commands you type still show.
First-run notice covers all of the above in 7 steps.
